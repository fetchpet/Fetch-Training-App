Rung — prototype

A dog training tracker built on how a dog actually learns something, rather than a library of lessons.

What changed in this pass

  • Opens on one thing, not a list. Charlie's face and a single instruction.
  • One tap to log. Three buttons on the home card. No drilling in.
  • The reveal is an event. Clear a rung and the screen goes black and plays the two clips together. It happens to you.
  • The bad-day button. "Feeling like it's not working?" plays every then-and-now, oldest first.
